Window Shake Snow Globe

Grab the window and shake it

Make the browser window small, then drag it around quickly. The page reads the window's screen position every frame and throws the snow the opposite way. You can also fling the globe itself with your mouse or finger.

shake with window 0, 0 speed 0 px/s shake

If "window" stays at 0, 0 while you drag, your browser hides the window position (Linux on Wayland and some privacy modes do this). Fling the globe instead.
